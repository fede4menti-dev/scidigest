SciDigest Privacy Policy

SciDigest does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • No account, sign-in, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • The app downloads the daily report files from this website (hosted on GitHub Pages). Like any website, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Your settings (report address, reminder time) and downloaded reports are stored only on your device.
  • Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is used.
  • Links to papers open in your browser and are subject to those websites' own policies.

Summaries in SciDigest are written by an AI model from publicly available abstracts and open-access papers, and may contain mistakes. Always check the original paper.
