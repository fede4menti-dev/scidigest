SciDigest Privacy Policy

SciDigest collects as little as possible. No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

Without an account

  • Your settings (topics, appearance, text size), saved papers, reading history and idea checks are stored only on your device (in your browser, or in the iPhone app).
  • Reports are downloaded from this website (hosted on GitHub Pages). Like any website, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Daily reminders in the iPhone app are local notifications on your device. No push server is used.

With an account (optional)

  • We store your email address, your password as a one-way secure hash (PBKDF2), a hash of your recovery code, and the data you choose to sync: topics, appearance and reading settings, saved papers, which papers you opened, and your recent idea checks.
  • This is stored on Cloudflare (Workers KV) and used only to sync your data between your devices.
  • You can export your data and delete your account at any time in Settings. Deleting your account removes it and its synced data from the server immediately.

Idea Check

  • Questions you check are sent to our server, which searches OpenAlex and Europe PMC and asks an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to write the report. The question and report are cached for up to 30 days, without any link to who asked, so identical questions are answered instantly.
  • To prevent abuse, the server counts requests per IP address for up to one hour; this count is not stored.

Links and summaries

  • Links to papers and images open other websites, which have their own policies.
  • Summaries are written by an AI model from publicly available abstracts and open-access papers and may contain mistakes. Always check the original paper.

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/fede4menti-dev/scidigest.
